Popify Bot - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 03 December 2024 | Last Updated: 25 September 2025

1. Information We Collect

User IDs, Server IDs, message data (only when required), voice connection details, and usage statistics.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide features (music, moderation, utilities).
  • To improve performance and fix bugs.
  • To prevent abuse or spam.
  • To comply with Discord’s policies.

3. Data Storage

Data is stored securely and only as long as needed. Unnecessary data is cleared automatically.

4. Data We Do Not Collect

No passwords, private DMs, or sensitive information is collected.

5. Data Retention

Temporary data (queues, logs) is cleared after use. Server settings remain until reset or removal.

6. Data Deletion Request

Removing the bot from your server deletes stored settings. You may also request deletion anytime.

7. Security Measures

Encrypted databases, restricted developer access, and regular audits for safety.

8. Compliance with Discord Policies

We strictly follow Discord Developer Terms and Community Guidelines.

9. Compliance with Laws

We comply with GDPR and CCPA. Users under 13 are not allowed.

10. Communication

Updates may be announced in the support server. No spam or ads are sent.
