Popify — Privacy Policy

What Data Does Popify Collect?

Popify only collects minimal data necessary for the bot to function properly. We do not collect personal or sensitive data.

Collected DataUsage & Purpose
Server IDsUsed to manage server-specific settings such as prefixes, playlists, and configurations.
User IDs (optional)Stored only when personal playlists or preferences are enabled by the user.

Do We Share Your Data?

No. Popify does not share, sell, or distribute any data to third parties. Your information stays private and secure.

How Can You Delete Your Data?

  1. Contact the Developer: Message Mr Johnny (mrjohnny.351) on Discord with your User ID or Server ID. Your data will be deleted as soon as possible.
  2. Join the Support Server: Use the Support Server to request deletion. Go to #chat, explain your issue, and our team will respond within 20 minutes.
  3. In-Bot Options: Some settings include options to reset or delete stored data. If unavailable, please use one of the two methods above.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Popify adds new features. Important changes will always be announced in the Support Server.
